1.1 Typography

1.1.1 A single versatile font

The first paragraph after a heading is set flush left. Body text is justified and hyphenated, so give the document a lang attribute or the hyphenator stays asleep. Acronyms like HTML and CSS are wrapped in <abbr> and come out as small caps; proper nouns you want to distinguish, such as Alegreya, take the canonical-name class.

Every following paragraph is indented three characters with no vertical gap between them, exactly as a book would do it. Long or awkward words can be nudged with soft hyphens, like inter­nationali­zation, so the justification engine has somewhere to break.

An aside sits in the right margin on wide screens, aligned to the paragraph above it, and collapses inline with a ❧ on narrow ones.

The paragraph after an aside continues the flow as though the aside were not there. Emphasis is italic; strong emphasis is heavier. Numerals are oldstyle by default: 1657, 2002, 2026.
